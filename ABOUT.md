# Why i created Resenhudo?

Some friends and i always finded out that some bots are EXTREMLY boring, and we wanted more than just a regular a bot: a FRIEND.

So, i developed this thinking first about the most collest feature: TRIGGERS. Imagine this: you is casually talking with some of your friend in your discord server. Then, someone say something and BOOM! The bot answer with an amazing thing (like a gif, a music, a video and etc)!
